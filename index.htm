How to use Task Board

  1. Add a task with “+ Add a task”. Only the name is required. Priority defaults to Medium and the due date to none.
  2. Priority levels are worth 5 to 1 points: Urgent (red), High (orange), Medium (yellow), Low (light green), Optional (light blue). The colour shows as a dot before the task name.
  3. The list starts sorted by due date (soonest first). Tasks with the same due date are ordered by priority. Click any column header to sort ascending or descending.
  4. Timer: press ▶ to start tracking time on a task and ⏸ to pause. Under the button, blue is your estimate and black is the time spent so far. Time keeps running until you pause.
  5. Edit: click anywhere on a row (except the checkbox and timer button) to change name, priority, due date and estimated duration (in hours and minutes). Save keeps changes; Cancel discards them.
  6. Complete a task with its checkbox. It moves to Finished tasks, newest first. Untick it there to send it back.
  7. Backup: Export shows all your data to copy and keep. Import restores it from a file or pasted text.
