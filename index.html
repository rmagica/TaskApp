How to use: Current tasks

  1. Add a task with the “+ Add a task” button below the list. Only the name is required. Priority defaults to Medium and the due date to none.
  2. Priority is worth 5 to 1 points: Urgent (red), High (orange), Medium (yellow), Low (light green), Optional (light blue). It shows as a dot before the name, or as two circling arrows for recurring tasks.
  3. The list is sorted by due date, then priority. Click any header to sort ascending or descending. Task & Due shows days left above the task name. 0 days (red) means due today or overdue.
  4. Timer: ▶ starts and ⏸ pauses, in the first column. Blue is your estimate and black is the time spent.
  5. Edit by clicking a row (not the checkbox or timer). Set priority, due date, estimated and actual duration, recurring (daily, weekly, monthly), Passive Time (a slider sets what percentage of the task’s time is passive), a category and notes. Save keeps changes, Cancel discards them, and the red trash icon deletes.
  6. Done? moves a task to Finished tasks. A recurring task comes back as a fresh copy at midnight on its next date.
  7. The totals above the list show tasks left (coloured by how many), and the estimated time left split between active and passive time, including the active time left on tasks due today.
  8. Backup: Export shows your data to copy. Import restores it from a file or pasted text.
  1. Finished tasks are listed newest first. Due & Done shows the due date in blue over the date you finished. Click any header to sort.
  2. Untick Done? to send a task back to Current tasks. Click a row to edit any detail, including the actual time.
  3. A finished recurring task stays here until its next date. Then a fresh copy appears in Current tasks and this entry stops recurring.
  4. The three averages cover the last 7 days (days with no tracked time or completions are left out). Tap the graph icon in front of one to see its 7-day chart.
  5. By Category switches the list to totals per category. Hrs is the total time spent, and the blue line fills at 100 hours. Tasks without a category are grouped as Uncategorised.
  6. Tap a category to show its own 7-day averages and graphs above the table. Tap it again to go back to all tasks. Press By Category again to return to the task list.
  7. Backup: Export shows your data to copy. Import restores it from a file or pasted text.
