How to use Task Board

  1. Add a task with “+ Add a task”. Only the name is required. Priority defaults to Medium and the due date to none.
  2. Priority is worth 5 to 1 points: Urgent (red), High (orange), Medium (yellow), Low (light green), Optional (light blue). It shows as a dot before the name, or as two circling arrows in a circle for recurring tasks.
  3. The list is sorted by due date, then priority. Click any header to sort ascending or descending.
  4. Task & Due shows days left above the task name. 0 days (red) means due today or overdue.
  5. Timer: ▶ starts and ⏸ pauses, in the first column. Blue is your estimate and black is the time spent.
  6. Edit by clicking a row (not the checkbox or timer). Set priority, due date, estimated and actual duration, recurring (daily, weekly, monthly), passive and notes. Save keeps changes, Cancel discards them, and the red trash icon deletes.
  7. Done? moves a task to Finished tasks, newest first. Untick it there to bring it back. Recurring tasks leave a history entry (only its actual duration can be edited) and reappear at midnight on their next date.
  8. The totals above the list split estimated time between active and passive tasks. On Finished tasks, the 7-day averages each have a graph icon that opens a chart.
  9. Backup: Export shows your data to copy. Import restores it from a file or pasted text.
